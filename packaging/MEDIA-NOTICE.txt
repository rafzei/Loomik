Loomik media tools
==================

Loomik invokes the separate FFmpeg and FFprobe executables through command-line
pipes. These executables statically include x264 and are distributed under the
GNU General Public License version 2 or later. Loomik's own source is MIT licensed.

FFmpeg: https://ffmpeg.org/ — copyright the FFmpeg developers.
x264: https://www.videolan.org/developers/x264.html — copyright the x264 developers.

The licenses directory contains the applicable license texts. The sources
directory contains the complete, unmodified source archives used for this build,
their SHA-256 hashes and original URLs (media-sources.env), the build recipe
(build-media-tools.sh), and generated build configuration files. Build-info.txt
records compiler/version/configuration information. No source modifications are
made by the recipe. These materials travel with the binary distribution; no
time-limited Actions artifact or third-party binary download is needed to obtain
the corresponding source.

To rebuild, use the Loomik repository layout, place the archives under
target/media-sources/, place the recipe under scripts/ and the source manifest
under packaging/, then follow docs/media-tools.md. You may modify and rebuild
these separate executables under their licenses. LOOMIK_FFMPEG and LOOMIK_FFPROBE
select replacement executables without modifying Loomik.
